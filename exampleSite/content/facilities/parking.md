Padma Mandapam offers on-site parking for up to 50 cars and 100 two-wheelers, with valet service available for weddings and large events.

## Details

- Covered parking for 50 cars across two basement levels
- Dedicated two-wheeler parking area
- Valet parking service for events over 200 guests
- Well-lit with 24-hour CCTV surveillance

## Nearby Options

For larger events, additional parking is available at the Mylapore municipal lot, a two-minute walk from the venue. Our staff can coordinate overflow parking and shuttle arrangements on request.
