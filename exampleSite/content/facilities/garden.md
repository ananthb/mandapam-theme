The rooftop terrace offers an open-air space for pre-wedding gatherings, cocktail receptions, and mehendi functions. With a view of the Mylapore skyline, it is a popular choice for evening events.

## Space

- Open-air terrace seating 150 guests
- Covered canopy area for shade and light rain
- Built-in lighting for evening events
- Separate service lift for catering access

## Best For

The terrace works well for sangeeth and mehendi functions, cocktail hours before a reception, or standalone evening events. It can be booked alongside the Grand Hall or independently.
